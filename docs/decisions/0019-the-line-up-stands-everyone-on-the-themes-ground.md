# 0019 The Line-up stands everyone on the theme's ground

Accepted 2026-11-01

Over a screen share, the start line of
[0016](0016-a-line-up-can-draw-the-shows-start.md) read as a crowd: racers
overlap at the line, the dice and pins sit far from the names, and the panel
covers the track.

- No line-up draws its show's start. Everyone stands in one row, in their
  look, over a card with their name, a die and a pin. A theme names its
  ground, a sky and a floor colour by token, and the row stands on it.
- The themes come first, above the row. A first-option choice that suits the
  chosen theme ([0017](0017-a-theme-names-the-choices-that-suit-it.md)) is
  starred, and one person's look opens in a sheet that shows those choices
  first.
- Shuffle, the dice and "Surprise me" deal the first option from the theme's
  choices or from all of them: a switch kept per show, on the theme's by
  default.
- The show names its themes and that option's choices for these words (The
  Race: "Track", "racers").

Still as 0016 decided: pins, and Shuffle leaving pinned looks alone.

Enforced by `specs/line-up.feature`.
